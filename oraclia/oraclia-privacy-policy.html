Oraclia Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Oleksandr Klymenko ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Oraclia (the "App").

Oraclia is a personal-divination app. It helps users record their dreams and the symbolic signs they notice during the day, and weaves those entries into evolving, time-bound prophecies about their near future. Oraclia is intended for personal reflection and entertainment. It is not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Account information, such as your Firebase user ID, sign-in method, email address, and profile information made available by the authentication provider you choose.
  • Seeker profile data you choose to share during the onboarding ritual or later under Profile → Personal information, so Oraclia can personalize your readings. A few answers are required: a name to be called by (any name or nickname you like) and an answer to the gender, relationship and children questions, each of which offers "Prefer not to say"; every other answer, including your date of birth, is optional. The profile may include: the name you want to be called, gender, date of birth and the zodiac sign and age derived from it, relationship status, whether you have children, eye color; your sleep habits (bedtime, falling asleep, night waking, how rested you feel); your inner weather (how often you feel stress or sadness, how often you do things that bring you joy, mental-health conditions that have been named in your life, whether you currently live with symptoms of them, significant recent life events); when you record your signs; your dream patterns (recurring dreams, nightmares, how your mornings feel); and what brought you to Oraclia. Until you create an account, these answers are kept only on your device; once you sign in they are stored in your user record so they follow you across devices, and you can change any of them, and clear the optional ones, at any time under Profile → Personal information.
  • Narrative content you create in the App, including dream entries, omen entries, prophecy records, personal interpretations, and the textual signs and reflections you log.
  • Engagement data, such as Seeker Rank, Insight Points, streak-related data, prophecy lifespans, reinforcement events, and similar progression state.
  • App settings and preference data, such as locale, notification settings, onboarding flags, reminder configuration, and similar user-level configuration.
  • Your device's time-zone offset (its difference from UTC, not your location), stored with each sign you record and sent when you edit one, so Oraclia counts a prophecy's window of manifestation in your own days.
  • The times of the readings you started during the last day, each with the sign it was for, kept only to limit how many readings one account can start in a day; older entries are dropped as new readings are counted, and the record is deleted with your account.
  • Subscription and donation data, such as subscription state, trial timing, purchase product identifiers, store source, and renewal/expiration timestamps needed to manage paid access.
  • Diagnostics and usage data, such as analytics events, crash reports, device/app version data, and limited technical information used to operate and improve the App.
  • Support information, including encrypted support-log bundles that you choose to export, share, or send when contacting support.

Oraclia also supports anonymous sign-in. If you use the App without creating a full account first, we may still associate your data with an anonymous Firebase account until you sign in with another method or delete that account.

How We Collect Information

We collect information when you:

  • sign in with email/password, Google, Apple, Facebook, or anonymous auth,
  • complete the onboarding ritual that builds your seeker profile or edit your answers under Profile → Personal information,
  • log dreams, omens, signs, or other narrative entries,
  • receive, reinforce, reinterpret, or close prophecies,
  • enable notifications and reminder-related features,
  • start, restore, redeem, or manage a subscription or donation flow,
  • use the App and generate analytics or crash-reporting events,
  • contact support or export logs.

The App does not collect precise device location.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • create and maintain your account,
  • save and sync your seeker profile, dream and omen entries, and prophecy history,
  • generate and evolve symbolic prophecies and personal interpretations — your sign entries, your related earlier signs, your living prophecies, the seeker profile answers you shared, and your Seeker Rank are sent together to our AI interpretation provider so each reading is written for you; unanswered profile fields are simply omitted,
  • schedule reminders, messages from Oraclia, and other opt-in notifications,
  • deliver subscription, donation, restore, and redemption features,
  • improve reliability, diagnose crashes, and understand feature usage,
  • respond to support requests and investigate reported problems,
  • comply with legal obligations and protect the App, users, and our rights.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your dream or omen entries or your seeker profile to train external machine-learning models outside what is required to operate the App.

Third-Party Services

Oraclia relies on third-party services that process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Depending on the platform and the features you use, these may include:

  • Firebase Auth
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase App Check (confirms that requests to our servers come from the genuine App: on iPhone through Apple's App Attest, on Android through Google Play Integrity; these check the device and the App installation and give Firebase a signed verdict, which carries none of your content or account details)
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Play Services
  • Google Sign-In
  • Sign in with Apple
  • Facebook Login (when you choose Continue with Facebook, Facebook shares with the App your name, your email address if your Facebook account has one, and an identifier for you that only this App sees; on iPhone the App asks Facebook for Limited Login, and on every platform it turns off the Facebook SDK's automatic app-event logging and advertising-identifier collection — Oraclia does not use Facebook for advertising or tracking)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play billing and subscription infrastructure
  • RevenueCat (subscription receipt validation, restore, and entitlement management; the App passes your Firebase user id to RevenueCat as the subscription owner identifier)
  • OpenAI (the AI models that validate, summarize, and interpret your signs run through OpenAI's API from our Cloud Functions; the text of the sign, summaries of your related earlier signs, your living prophecies, the seeker profile answers you chose to share, and your Seeker Rank are sent as the reading's context, without your name on the account, email address, or Firebase user id)
  • Your device's speech recognition (Apple's speech recognition on iPhone, the device's recognition service on Android), when you dictate a sign instead of typing it: it turns your voice into text and may process the audio on its provider's servers. The App receives only the text and does not record or store audio.

These providers may process information needed to authenticate you, store your App data, deliver and validate subscriptions, or provide analytics, diagnostics, and platform services.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Oraclia stores notification preferences and scheduling-related data so the App can deliver messages from Oraclia, prophecy updates, dream-logging reminders, and similar communications. Notification delivery may also depend on Apple, Google, and device-level notification services. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Sharing Prophecy Cards

If you share a prophecy card, the App draws the card's face as an image on your device and hands it, with a short line of text, to your device's share sheet. Where it goes is your choice, and the app or service you pick handles it under its own terms. Saving the image to your photos asks for add-only access to your photo library; the App never reads your photo library.

Support Logs

If you choose to export or share support logs, the App prepares an encrypted support bundle before sharing it. These bundles may include technical diagnostics, app version information, device details, and redacted log content needed to troubleshoot issues.

Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to operate the App, support your account, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Some data may also remain in backups, logs, or service-provider systems for a limited period according to operational or legal requirements.

Account Deletion and Your Choices

You can stop future collection by uninstalling the App.

You can also delete your account from inside the App at any time. The Profile screen exposes a "Delete account" action. Confirming it deletes everything stored under your account — your seeker profile, every dream, omen and sign you recorded with its readings, your prophecies, your preferences and drafts — and then your Firebase Auth account. To make sure nothing can be written back to a deleted account from another device that is still signed in to it, we keep only a record of the account's random identifier and the time it was deleted, with none of your content. The flow requires you to reauthorize before the deletion is performed (with Google, Apple, Facebook, or your email and password, depending on how you sign in) so that no one else can delete your account from an unattended device; our server carries out the deletion only right after you have signed in again. If you sign in with Apple, deleting your account in the iPhone app first withdraws Oraclia's access to your Apple ID (Sign in with Apple), so Oraclia no longer appears among the apps using your Apple ID; Apple may email you about it. Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are retained as their terms and the law require.

If you signed in with Facebook, deleting your account removes everything Oraclia stored, as described above. Facebook keeps its own record that you used it to sign in to Oraclia; to remove Oraclia from your Facebook account, open Facebook's Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, choose Oraclia and remove it.

The Profile screen also exposes an "Erase all readings" action that wipes your accumulated divination data (every dream, omen, sign, prophecy, and the symbolic vector embeddings derived from them) while keeping your sign-in account active. Confirming this action triggers a server-side process that batch-deletes the relevant records from our database. Your account itself, your sign-in method, and your in-app preferences (such as language) are not affected; you can continue using Oraclia immediately and begin a fresh divination history. This action is irreversible — once your past readings are erased, we cannot restore them.

If you need help with additional deletion, export, or other privacy requests, contact us at oraclia.help@gmail.com.
